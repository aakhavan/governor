# C6: KPI catalogue, layer blueprint and approved-decisions formats

Capstone batch 1, step 6. Contracts: `docs/capstone/design.md` §6, §7 and §8; vocabulary in `CONTEXT.md`.

Build:
1. `src/governor/core/catalogue.py`: Pydantic models and loaders `load_kpis(path)`, `load_blueprint(path)`, `load_approved(path)`. The models mirror the YAML keys, so `model_dump()` returns the documented structure (for example `catalogue.kpis`, `blueprint.layers["silver"].dbt`).
   - KPI catalogue validation: unique ids; `status` is `proposed` or `approved`; every `{placeholder}` in `template_sql` is a declared relation or input; every input names a declared relation; `requires` lists declared relations only. Errors name the KPI id and the problem.
   - `render(kpi, mapping) -> str`: fills the template from a mapping of relation and input names to SQL identifiers or expressions; refuses a mapping that leaves a placeholder unfilled or adds unknown names.
   - Blueprint: parse the YAML front matter of `knowledge/blueprint.md` (layers with their dbt folders, rules with id, severity and text, exceptions); validate rule ids are unique and severities are high, medium or low.
   - Approved decisions: validate `kind` is one of the three kinds and every entry has a reason, an approver and a date.
2. `knowledge/blueprint.md`: front matter with the three layers and rules L1 to L9 exactly as in design §7, an empty exceptions list, then a short prose section explaining each layer and rule in plain words.
3. `knowledge/kpis.yaml`: `version: 1` and an empty `kpis: []` list, with a header comment saying the definitions are added after Amir approves them. Do not invent KPI definitions.
4. `knowledge/approved.yaml`: `version: 1` and `decisions: []`, with a header comment.
5. Tests in `tests/core/test_catalogue.py` with their own fixtures (including the AOV example from design §6): a valid catalogue loads; each validation error is caught; `render` fills and refuses as specified; the real blueprint, catalogue and approved files load.

Read: `docs/capstone/design.md` §6 to §8, `CONTEXT.md`.

Done when `plan/accept/accept_c6.py` passes. This step has a human gate: Amir approves the blueprint wording.
